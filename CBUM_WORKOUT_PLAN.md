## Core Training Principles

### 1. 📈 Double Progression

Work in a set rep range. Once you hit the top for all sets, increase weight next session. **Reps first, then weight.**

### 2. ⚡ German Volume Training (GVT)

- **10 sets × 10 reps** with minimal rest
- Used on: Machine Chest Press (Day 1) and Chest-Supported Rows (Day 2)
- Brutal volume stimulus for massive growth

### 3. 🔗 Tri-Sets & Supersets

- Move exercise-to-exercise with **NO rest within the cluster**
- Rest only between full rounds
- Maximizes pump and time efficiency

### 4. 🐢 Slow Eccentrics

- **3-4 seconds down**, 1s pause at stretch, explode up
- Especially emphasized on incline press
- More time under tension = more hypertrophy

### 5. 🧠 Mind-Muscle Connection

- Quality reps over ego weight
- Feel the target muscle working
- Hard contraction at the top of every rep

### 6. 📏 Extended Range of Motion

- Full stretch at the bottom
- Full contraction at the top
- CBum's 2024 emphasis across ALL muscle groups

### 7. 🚫 No Barbell Squats

- CBum dropped these to stay pain-free as he ages
- Belt squat, hack squat, and pendulum squat replace them
- Zero spine load = sustainable training

### 8. 💪 Lagging Muscle Focus

- **Biceps + Calves** get special attention
- Fat Gripz for thickness
- Extra volume and intensity techniques built in

## Training Tags Reference

- **GVT** — German Volume Training (10×10 with minimal rest)
- **TRI-SET A/B/C** — Cluster sets with no rest within group
- **SUPERSET A/B/C** — Agonist-antagonist pairs (antagonistic muscles)
- **SLOW ECCENTRIC** — Emphasis on eccentric phase (3-4 second descent)
- **STRAIGHT SETS** — Standard sets with rest between sets
- **⭐ Priority** — Primary/focal exercise for the day

## Program Adaptations & Notes

### For Other Athletes

CBum is a 6× Mr. Olympia champion with **full-time recovery**, sponsorship support, and potentially pharmacological advantages.

**What IS transferable:**

- ✅ Program structure and principles
- ✅ Tri-sets, double progression, slow eccentrics
- ✅ Extended range of motion emphasis
- ✅ Mind-muscle connection focus
- ✅ Exercise selection philosophy

**How to adapt:**

1. **Start with straight sets** before adding GVT
2. **Ease into 10×10 over 4–6 weeks** to build capacity
3. **Prioritize recovery** — ensure 7-8 hours sleep
4. **Track progression** using the double progression principle
5. **Listen to your body** — adapt exercise selection for joint health
6. **Don't ego lift** — quality over quantity
7. **Consider equipment availability** — use provided alternatives

## Key Takeaways

### Lagging Muscle Development

- **Biceps:** Multiple curl variations, Fat Gripz emphasis, high frequency (4×/cycle)
- **Calves:** Multiple angles, high rep ranges (15–20), straight sets for intensity

### Joint Health

- No barbell squats (zero spine compression)
- Emphasis on chest-supported and machine-based exercises
- Full ROM with controlled tempos

### Intensity Techniques Built-In

- GVT on large compound movements (chest press, rows)
- Tri-sets for metabolic stress and pump
- Agonist-antagonist supersets for time efficiency

### Programming Philosophy

- **Double progression first** (reps before weight)
- **Extended ROM** as the 2024 emphasis
- **Mind-muscle connection** over absolute strength
- **Sustainable intensity** for longevity

## Notes for Analysis

This program represents Chris Bumstead's 2024 off-season approach focusing on:

1. Hypertrophy through varied volume and intensity
2. Joint-friendly alternatives (no barbell squats)
3. High frequency on lagging muscle groups
4. Extended range of motion for maximum stretch and contraction
