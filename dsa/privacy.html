🔒 Privacy and your data

🏦 Think of the hub as a lobby with lockers. Your locker is in your own GitHub account, and only your key opens it. The hub keeps no copy.

📦 What is stored

  1. On this site's server: nothing. There is no database and no account list.
  2. In your GitHub account: one secret gist named tracker-sync-v1 with your progress and notes, once you sign in.
  3. In this browser: your progress, notes, 3 undo snapshots and your sign-in key (a GitHub token).

👀 Who can see it

  1. Only you. Other users of the hub cannot read or change your gist.
  2. A GitHub "secret" gist is hidden, not encrypted: anyone who has its exact link can open it. Never put passwords or secrets in your notes.
  3. Sign-in asks GitHub for the gist permission, the narrowest one GitHub offers. It is used only for the tracker gist.

🚪 Signing out

  1. Tap your @username button to sign out.
  2. Signing out cancels your sign-in key at GitHub, so a copied key stops working.
  3. On a shared or public computer, choose erase when asked, so the next person can't read your notes.
  4. You can also cancel the hub's access any time at github.com/settings/applications.

🗑 Delete my data

  1. Deletes your tracker-sync-v1 gist from GitHub.
  2. Cancels your sign-in key.
  3. Erases all hub data from this browser.
  4. Other devices keep their own copy until you erase them too.
