📖 How to use

Two minutes to read. Everything else in the hub builds on this.

🧭 What it is

  1. Over 600 interview problems, grouped by the pattern that solves them.
  2. A memory coach that tells you which problems to look at again, and when.
  3. Your progress stays in this browser, and follows you to other devices after Sign in with GitHub.

🏋️ Think of a gym coach. You lift the weight (solve a problem). The coach decides when you lift it again: just before your muscle (memory) would forget it.

🔁 How revision works

  1. Solve a problem on LeetCode.
  2. Mark it ✅ Solved on its card.
  3. When it comes back, read only the problem and recall the approach in your head.
  4. Grade yourself honestly: ✓ solid, ~ shaky or ✗ forgot.
  5. The tracker picks the next day it comes back.
✓ solidComes back later each time: 1, 2, 4, 8, then 16 days.
~ shakyComes back in 2 days.
✗ forgotComes back tomorrow, and joins ⌨️ "to code": write the code again from scratch.

🃏 Like flashcards: the cards you know drift to the back of the pile, the ones you miss stay on top.

📅 Every day

  1. Open 🏠 Dashboard.
  2. Clear the Today list: recall and grade each question that is due.
  3. Do the Question of the day.
  4. Learn one new pattern: open a topic, read its cheat sheet, solve a few of its problems.
