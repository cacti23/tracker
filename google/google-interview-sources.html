🎯 Google Interview Sources

Links, not questions — problems rotate, patterns don't. Optimized for freshness + first-person detail + an actual interview date + concrete round/problem information. A source is self-reported unless stated otherwise — "candidate wrote this," not "this is confirmed accurate."

⚠️ Round structure varies Don't assume one fixed Google L4/SWE interview structure from another candidate's report. Round count and focus can vary by role, location, and requisition. Your recruiter's instructions take precedence over any post below.
🗓️ Your cadence + the freshness rule One write-up every 1–2 days is plenty. Prioritize interviews from the last 12 months — 2026 over 2025 — and note the actual interview date stated in the post, not when it was published or updated. For each one, write down: (1) the pattern, in your cheatsheets' vocabulary, not the LC number, (2) solid or a gap for you, (3) one line on what tripped the candidate up.

Tier 3 — official (current process, not candidate experience)

Tells you how Google itself describes the process right now — round count, evaluation criteria, timeline. It will never tell you what problem you'll get.

🏛️ Google Careers — Our hiring process

Google's own page: recruiter screen → phone screen → onsite loop (2–3 DSA rounds + a behavioral round, system design added at senior levels) → hiring committee review, evaluated on General Cognitive Ability, Role-Related Knowledge, Leadership and Googleyness. Six to eight weeks end to end, typically.
