# Program for review: 5-Day PPL ×2 + Legs (Rambod/C Bum hybrid)

## Lifter
6'0" (183cm), ~88kg, natural, trains 5x/week, goal = max muscle in optimal time. Wants heavy compound lifts, likes FST-7 finishers, likes a Week A/B accessory rotation, dislikes exercise repetition across the week.

## Structure
Push A → Pull A → Rest → Push B → Pull B → Legs (Sat) → Rest. Every muscle trained 2×/week: one **heavy** session (5-8 reps, lean exercise count, just compounds + one FST-7 finisher) and one **pump** session (10-20 reps, superset-heavy, carries that muscle's isolation volume for the week). Primary lifts are constant every week for progressive overload; only accessories swap Week A ↔ Week B. FST-7 = 7 sets × 10-15 reps, 30-45s rest, one per session — and weight on FST-7 sets is meant to progress over time too, not stay fixed.

## Weekly frequency per muscle (direct isolation work, excluding compound-lift carryover)
- Chest: 2× (Push A heavy, Push B pump)
- Delts (side): 1× direct isolation (Push B) + heavy indirect from 4 pressing movements/week
- Triceps: 2× (Push B, Pull B) + indirect from all pressing
- Back (width/thickness): 2× (Pull A, Pull B)
- Hamstrings: 2× (Pull A conventional deadlift, Legs stiff-leg deadlift)
- Biceps: 1× session but 3 total movements (Pull A: 1, Pull B: 2) + indirect from rows/pull-ups
- Rear delts: 1× direct (Face Pull, Push B) + heavy indirect from 4 pulling movements/week
- Quads: 2× (Push A leg press, Legs squat)
- Calves: 2× (Push B standing, Legs seated)
- Abs: 2× (Pull A, Pull B)

## Known trade-off
Delts, rear delts, and biceps get only 1 direct isolation session/week instead of 2, on the reasoning that they already get substantial indirect stimulus from the heavy compounds (pressing for delts, rowing/pulling for rear delts and biceps) and that concentrating their isolation volume into one pump session avoids diluting the heavy days. Open question for review: is 1×/week direct isolation + indirect compound work actually enough for these muscles, or should one of them get a token set added back to its "off" day?

## What I want critiqued
1. Is the heavy/pump day split (lean compounds+FST-7 vs. full isolation volume) sound, or does concentrating a muscle's isolation work into one session per week create a recovery/stimulus problem vs. spreading it thinner across two sessions?
2. Is the conventional deadlift placement (Pull A, 4×5, same week as heavy squat on Saturday) safe for weekly CNS/lower-back recovery, or too much combined axial loading across the week?
3. Any exercise selection or rep-range red flags for a natural, ~88kg, intermediate-to-advanced lifter chasing hypertrophy with heavy compounds as the priority?
