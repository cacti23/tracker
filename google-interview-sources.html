🎯 Google Interview Sources

Links only, no questions listed here — problems rotate, patterns don't. 2025/2026 only. Every source below was checked, not just linked.

🗓️ Your cadence One interview write-up every 1–2 days is plenty. Read it once, then write your own note answering three things: (1) what pattern was this, in the vocabulary your cheatsheets use — not the LC number, (2) is that pattern already solid for you or a gap, (3) one line on what tripped the candidate up, if they said. That third one is usually the most useful line in the whole post.

Tier 1 — read these first

Dated, candidate-written, updated continuously. This is where your daily read should come from.

📄 LeetCode Discuss checked

The single best free source. Real candidates post within days of their actual loop — noisy prose, not a clean table, and some withhold specifics for NDA reasons, but this is as close to ground truth as free gets.

  • Start here → 📌 Google Latest Interview Experiences — Collection — the curated master index, sorted by phone screen / onsite / level.
  • Snapshot → Google Latest Interview Experiences (March 2025) — a dated compilation, useful to gauge how far back the master index's "recent" goes.
  • Second angle → Google Interview Experiences — Compilation — an independent thread, different posters, different overlap.

🌱 Taro checked

A real tech-career community, not a content mill. Individually dated write-ups tagged by role, level, location and outcome. Verified live entries from June, October and November 2025.

  • Hub → Google Interview Questions & Experiences — frequency-ranked question list plus a full-experiences tab.
  • Experiences only → Google Interview Experiences tab — the dated write-ups directly.
🔎 Do this yourself too Neither site can be fully captured in a bookmark — new posts land continuously. Every couple of weeks, go to leetcode.com/discuss or Taro's Google page and search/sort by newest for Google interview, Google L4, Google phone screen. That live check is worth more than any static link, including the ones above.

Tier 2 — supplementary

Real, but noisier or less consistently dated. Use to cross-check Tier 1, not as your primary read.

💬 Teamblind (Blind) checked

Identity-verified-at-signup (corporate email), so it skews toward real current/ex-Google employees, not SEO writers. Format is forum threads, not structured write-ups.

  • Google Interview Questions board
  • Google company page — "google interview" posts

🏢 Glassdoor checked

Anonymous, crowdsourced, but genuinely dated — confirmed live entries from September 2026 and August 2025. Quality varies a lot: some posts name the exact topic ("graph, array, DP round"), many are vague ("one medium-hard question, don't remember"). Skim for the dated ones, skip the vague ones.

  • Google Software Engineer — Interview Questions

🚫 Left out, and why

  • PracHub, Hack2Hire, Jobaaj, Naukri Code360, Educative, Interview Kickstart — "Updated 2026" branding on every URL, no verifiable candidate sourcing behind it.
  • Aced / Exponent (tryexponent.com) — a paid coaching platform; "verified by hiring managers" isn't checkable, and individual questions carry no date. Same templated-freshness pattern as the ones above.
  • krishnadey30's GitHub CompanyWise repo — most-starred on GitHub, data is from mid-2024. Stars ≠ freshness.
  • snehasishroy GitHub company-wise dataset — a periodic scrape (LC numbers + frequency %), no date or story per question. Still fine for tagging questions — it's what powers this tracker's own company tags — just not for reading real accounts, which is what this page is for.
