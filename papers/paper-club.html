🌐 Where these come from (sites worth exploring)

  • Marc Brooker's blogAWS engineer behind #1. Short, sharp posts on retries, queues, databases.
  • Metadata (Murat Demirbas)Distributed-systems paper reviews, still active.

  • Designing Data-Intensive ApplicationsKleppmann's book. The best companion to this whole list.
  • Jepsen analysesWhat databases actually guarantee vs. what they claim.

  • Google Research publicationsOfficial home of GFS, MapReduce, Bigtable, Dapper and more.
  • Discord engineering blogWhere #4 comes from. Good real-world scaling stories.
