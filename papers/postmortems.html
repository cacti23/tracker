🔍 How to read a postmortem (4 questions)
  1. What was the trigger? The small change or event that started it.
  2. Why did it spread? What turned one fault into a big outage.
  3. Why did recovery take so long? Tools, access or knowledge that was missing.
  4. What did they change after? The fix you can reuse in your own systems.
